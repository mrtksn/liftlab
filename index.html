    Fly the real drone

    This page becomes the command module. Its program runs here and talks to the drone's ESP32-S3 or C3 over Bluetooth LE, set to radio=ble. The 3D view and the Ground tab show the drone's telemetry, and the simulation pauses while you're connected.

    • Binding phrase: (the Ground tab's). The drone's bind= must be the same.
    • Props off the first time. Arm and take off are switches on the bar over the view.
    • Keep this tab in front. A hidden tab slows to once a second, the channels stop, and the drone's failsafe takes over.

    This browser has no Web Bluetooth here. Use Chrome or Edge on a computer or Android (not Safari, Firefox or iOS), with the page served over https or from localhost.

    Allocation

    When a move can be made more than one way, these decide how. They never trade away any of the move itself. Each is a pull weighed against the others: 0 turns it off, 0.2 is the strongest (the defaults are 0.02, 0.02 and 0.01).
